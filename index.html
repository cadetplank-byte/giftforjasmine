my darling,

if you’re reading this, i imagine there’s a little part of you wishing i were there.

perhaps you wish i could hold your hand, kiss your forehead, or simply sit beside you and talk about absolutely nothing.

and god, how i wish i could.

i wish i could reach through these words and remind you that distance is only the space between us, not a measurement of how much i love you.

because even when i’m not beside you, there is so much of you that stays with me.

i carry you in the songs i hear, in the sunsets i stop to admire, in the little things that make me smile because i know you’d find them funny.

sometimes i look at the sky and wonder if you’re looking at it too.

whether the same colours are falling across your evening, whether you’ve noticed the clouds, whether you’ve stopped for a moment to appreciate something beautiful.

and then i think that perhaps we’re not quite as far apart as we feel.

if you miss me, remember this.

i miss the way you laugh.

not just the sound of it, but the way it seems to change a room. the way you can make something ordinary feel like a memory worth keeping.

i miss your voice, even when you’re talking about things that have absolutely no importance to anyone else in the world.

especially then, actually.

i miss your little mannerisms, your clumsiness, your expressions, the things you probably don’t even notice but that i would recognise anywhere.

i miss looking at you and thinking, how can someone be so effortlessly lovely?

and i hope you know that i don’t only miss the beautiful moments.

i miss the silly ones, too.

if you start doubting yourself, read this twice.

i wish you could see what i see.

not because i think you’re blind to your own beauty, but because i don’t think you could ever fully understand how much there is to love about you.

your eyes, like a forest just before the sun disappears, holding a thousand little shades that i could spend forever getting lost in.

your smile, which has absolutely no business being as distracting as it is.

your mind, which i could listen to for hours.

but more than anything, your heart.

the heart that cares, that gives, that notices, that loves in all the little ways people sometimes forget to appreciate.

please don’t ever believe you have to be anything other than yourself to be deserving of affection.

you don’t have to be elegant every second, you don’t have to be clever every moment, and you certainly don’t have to stop being clumsy just because i happen to find it adorable.

i want every version of you.

the happy one. the sleepy one. the quiet one. the overthinking one. the one who laughs at absolutely nothing.

all of them.

if you wish i were holding you, imagine this.

my arms around you, your head resting against me, the world becoming a little quieter.

no rushing. no worrying about tomorrow. no need to find something to say.

just us.

and i’d probably tell you that i love you.

then i’d tell you again, because once would never quite be enough.

i’d remind you that three months may seem like such a small chapter in the grand story of our lives, but it is a chapter i would never willingly erase.

there are so many things i still want to experience with you.

so many conversations we haven’t had.

so many sunsets we haven’t watched.

so many songs i haven’t heard you sing along to.

so many ordinary days that i haven’t yet had the privilege of spending with you.

and i hope, when you think about all the things ahead, you find yourself smiling at the possibility of them.

and if you still miss me after all that...

then let yourself miss me.

there is nothing wrong with wishing someone were closer.

but don’t let the distance convince you that you’re alone.

somewhere, there is a person who would choose your company over a thousand other places to be.

someone who wants to hear how your day went, what made you laugh, what song you’ve been listening to, and whether you’ve seen anything pretty today.

someone who thinks about you when the world goes quiet.

someone who wishes that every beautiful thing could somehow find its way to you.

that person is me.

and until i can tell you all of this without a page between us, i hope these words can do a little of the holding for me.

so read them whenever you need to.

read them when the nights feel long, when you wish i were closer, when you need a reminder of just how precious you are.

and remember, my one true love,

i may not always be within reach, but you are never far from my thoughts.

i miss you in all the little ways, i love you in all the big ones, and i cannot wait until missing you becomes holding you again.

always yours. ♡
